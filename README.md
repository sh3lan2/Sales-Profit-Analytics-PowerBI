## 🔍 Key Insights & Analysis

1. **Category Performance:** 
   * **Technology** leads in revenue generation ($704.45K / 36.36%), followed closely by **Furniture** ($622.34K / 28.59%) and **Office Supplies** ($602.09K / 27.66%).
2. **Yearly Sales Trends:**
   * Sustained year-over-year revenue growth from **2021 through 2023**, with peak performance in 2023 ($0.23M in Technology).
3. **Geographic Distribution:**
   * High customer concentration across major US metropolitan hubs with key contribution nodes along the East and West coasts.
4. **Customer Profitability:**
   * Top revenue-generating customers identified (led by *Sean Miller* at ~$25K sales), providing targets for VIP retention programs.
